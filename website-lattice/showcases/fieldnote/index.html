Our Story

Coffee worth
the walk

We roast in small batches and pull every shot to order. The bar is the same one that has been in this building since it was a hardware store — we just added better espresso.

Oat, whole, and local dairy. Pastries baked two doors down. A short list of beer and wine for the after-four crowd.
