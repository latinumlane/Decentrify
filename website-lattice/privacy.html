[ Privacy policy ]

Privacy without the fine print.

Decentrify is built to respect your attention and your information. This policy explains what we collect when you choose to contact us, why we collect it, and what we do not collect.

Effective August 17, 2026

No tracking.

We do not use analytics tools, advertising pixels, retargeting tags, device fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking. We do not create visitor profiles, sell personal information, or share personal information for targeted advertising. The site does not set analytics or advertising cookies.

Information you choose to provide.

We collect information only when you voluntarily contact us through a form, email, or phone. Depending on what you submit, that may include your name, company, email address, phone number, project needs, budget, timeline, and the message you send us.

How we use it.

We use submitted information to respond to your inquiry, understand the project you are asking about, and, if you choose to work with us, provide and manage our services. We do not use it for behavioral advertising or sell it to anyone.

Service providers and technical data.

Our website host and form-delivery providers may process information needed to operate the site, deliver a form submission, protect against abuse, or maintain security. Those providers act on our behalf and are not permitted to use your information for their own marketing. Like virtually all websites, hosting infrastructure may retain limited technical request logs for security and reliability; Decentrify does not use those logs to track visitors or identify them across sites.

Retention and security.

We keep inquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain our business records, or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect submitted information, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices.

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have provided, subject to applicable law and necessary recordkeeping. To make a request, email lane@decentrify.biz. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Age requirement.

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes and contact.

We may update this policy when our practices or legal obligations change. We will post the current version here and update the effective date. Questions about this policy can be sent to lane@decentrify.biz.
