You can explore this questionnaire and download your answers here. Online submission will be enabled on the published client site.

01 / Business & contactLet’s start with you.

Tell us who you are and how to get in touch. Only your name and email are required.

02 / Business overviewThe business behind the brief.

What do you sell, where do you operate, and who is your ideal customer?

03 / Goals & successWhat needs to change?

What are your top priorities, current obstacles, and measures of success?

04 / Services neededChoose your starting points.

Select any services you’re considering. It’s fine if you’re not sure yet.

05 / Existing assets & systemsWhat are we working with?

Link brand files, websites, or apps. Note your CMS, CRM, analytics, and relevant integrations.

06 / Scope & directionThe shape of the project.

List must-have features, pages, or deliverables. Add 1–2 reference brands or websites.

07 / Budget & engagementAn investment that fits.

Project budget or monthly service budget? Separate ad spend? Open to a three-month engagement?

08 / Timeline & approvalsWhen does it need to happen?

Desired kickoff and launch date? Who approves the work, and are there fixed deadlines?

09 / Marketing & operationsWhat’s happening today?

Which channels are active? What is working? For AI work, which workflow should improve first?

10 / Proposal requirementsAnything else we should know?

Any constraints, compliance needs, preferred payment structure, or other information?

11 / Review & sendYour brief, in one place.

Review your answers before sending. You can go back to change anything. Blank optional questions are fine.
