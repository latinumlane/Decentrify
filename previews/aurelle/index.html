I'm new to aesthetics. Where should I start?

Start with a consultation. It is a chance to discuss your goals and medical history, understand your options, and decide whether treatment is right for you. There is no obligation to proceed.

Can my results still look like me?

Our concept is built around a conservative, individual approach. A consultation would clarify realistic expectations, alternatives, and the limits of each treatment. Results cannot be guaranteed.

What is the difference between Botox and filler?

Botox is a prescription injectable used for selected expression lines. Dermal fillers address selected areas of volume or contour. They work differently and have distinct risks, which a qualified clinician would explain before treatment.

Will I need downtime?

Recovery varies by treatment and person. Redness, swelling, or bruising may occur. Your clinician would explain expected recovery and aftercare before you choose a treatment.
