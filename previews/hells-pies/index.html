02 / THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO DOOM

Detroit soul.
Dublin after dark.

Hell's Pies is Dublin’s first Detroit-style slice shop. Find us at 66 Ember Lane, Northbridge, Dublin 1, serving square pizza slices from lunchtime into the night.

Detroit-style means a thick, airy base, a rectangular pan and that glorious crisp cheese edge. It’s pizza with a little more backbone. Grab a classic Red Top, turn up the heat with Holy Pepperoni, or make your peace with pineapple.

Lunch break, city wander or the long way home: there’s a slice with your name on it.

QUESTIONS FROM THE LIVING

Before you bite.

Where can I get Detroit-style pizza in Dublin?

Hell's Pies is Dublin’s first Detroit-style slice shop, at 66 Ember Lane, Northbridge, Dublin 1, D02 XC91. Check the menu above or order through the online shop.

How late is Hell's Pies open?

The home and menu pages list noon to midnight Sunday to Thursday and noon to 3am Friday and Saturday. For holiday changes, check with the shop.

How much is a slice?

The published menu lists slices at €5–€6. The €13 meal deal includes two slices, one dip and one drink. Live prices and availability are shown in the online shop.

Can I check allergens before ordering?

Yes. Read Hell's Pies’s allergen list and speak to the team about dietary requirements before ordering. Don’t assume an item is suitable based only on its name.

Do you offer catering and gift cards?

For catering, email info@hellspies.example. Digital gift cards are available through the linked Square gift-card shop.
