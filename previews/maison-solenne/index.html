A few things to know

The details,
considered.

When can we check in and check out?

Our concept check-in is from 3pm, with checkout by 11am. Early arrival and late departure would be arranged subject to availability.

What is included in a stay?

The sample room offering includes breakfast for two, Wi-Fi, garden pool access and daily housekeeping. Spa treatments, restaurant dining and private transfers are priced separately.

Is there a pool and spa?

The imagined house features a seasonal outdoor pool and an intimate treatment space with botanical massages and facials. Treatments require advance reservations. Pool opening would depend on the season and weather.

Can you arrange transfers and parking?

The concept concierge can coordinate airport transfers and advise on local parking. Transfer fees and parking availability would be confirmed before arrival.

Are there accessible rooms and family options?

The planned room collection includes an accessible ground-floor option and selected suites suitable for a third guest. A real booking team would confirm step-free routes, room measurements and individual requirements before a reservation.

What is the cancellation policy?

This is a pitch concept, so no actual cancellation terms apply. In a live booking flow, the full cancellation deadline, deposit, taxes and total rate would be shown before payment.
