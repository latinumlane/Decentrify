THE STUDIO / INTERIOR INSPIRATION
Our Beverly Hills story

A private pause.
In the heart
of it all.

FORMA imagines a different kind of Beverly Hills med spa: an intimate, appointment-based practice where the pace slows, the conversation comes first, and the details feel quietly considered.

Conceived for the Golden Triangle neighborhood, the studio brings together the privacy of a personal consultation space and the ease of a small, design-led retreat. Soft light, clean lines, and a restrained palette create room to breathe before your treatment begins.

Our philosophy is simple. Aesthetic care should fit your life, respect your features, and make sense for your skin over time. Whether you are considering injectables for the first time, exploring skin renewal, or making space for a regular facial ritual, the starting point is always an honest assessment.

From the first conversation to follow-up care, the experience is designed around continuity. You understand the plan, the costs, the alternatives, and the expected recovery before you choose what comes next.

Before your visit

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Do I need to know which treatment I want?

No. A consultation is a place to begin, even if you are simply curious. Your skin, goals, and medical history would guide an informed conversation about suitable options.

Can I have treatment at my first appointment?

That depends on the service, your suitability, and availability. An assessment and informed consent come first. There is no obligation to proceed with treatment.

How do the introductory offers work?

These are sample offers for this concept: a $75 injectables consultation credited toward a suitable treatment within 30 days, a $50 skin planning consultation, and a $175 first-visit facial. They are not available to purchase.

Will I need recovery time?

Recovery differs by treatment and person. Temporary redness, swelling, or bruising may occur. A qualified clinician would explain treatment-specific risks and aftercare before you decide.

Is FORMA an operating Beverly Hills clinic?

No. FORMA, its doctor profile, offers, and location are fictional elements created for this website pitch. The booking flow is a local demo and does not send information.
