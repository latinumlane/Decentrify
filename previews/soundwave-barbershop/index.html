Where is SoundWave Barbershop barbershop in Dublin?

Find SoundWave Barbershop at SoundWave House, 42 Harbor Lane, Northbridge, Dublin 1, close to Blessington Street Basin. Use the map below to plan your route to the shop.

How do I book a haircut?

Book online through our booking desk. Choose your haircut or grooming service, check the available appointment times and complete your booking there. Your appointment is confirmed through our booking desk.

Do you offer skin fades in Northbridge?

Yes. Skin fades are listed on the our booking desk menu, alongside clipper haircuts and packages combining a skin fade with beard grooming. Select the service that matches what you want when booking.

Can I book a haircut and beard trim together?

Yes. our booking desk lists a Skin Fade + Beard package and a Skin Fade + Beard + Eyebrow package. Check the booking page for the current price and appointment duration.

How much is a men’s haircut?

our booking desk currently lists the men’s cut at €20 and the skin fade at €25. Some grooming services and packages have off-peak offers. Check your selected service and time on our booking desk for the final price.

What other grooming services are available?

The published menu includes beard trimming, eyebrow grooming, and nose and ear waxing, in addition to men’s haircuts and skin fades. Availability and prices are shown on our booking desk.

What are the opening hours?

Check our booking desk for current appointment availability before travelling. Online listings show different hours, so booking ahead is the clearest way to choose an available time.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Walk-in availability has not been confirmed. Book a time through our booking desk to arrange your visit rather than relying on a space being available when you arrive.

Which languages are spoken?

The our booking desk profile lists English, Romanian and Brazilian Portuguese. This can help you explain the length, shape and finish you want for your haircut.

Can I pay by card?

our booking desk lists cash, credit cards and debit cards as accepted payment methods. Check any booking payment requirements when you reserve your appointment.

What should I ask for when booking a fade?

Choose the skin fade service, then bring a reference photo or explain your preferred length on top and how high you want the fade. If you also want beard grooming, select the relevant combined package.

Is the shop near Blessington Street Basin?

Yes. SoundWave Barbershop is on Harbor Lane in Northbridge, and our booking desk lists the venue in the Blessington Street Basin area of Dublin. Follow the directions link for a route from your starting point.
