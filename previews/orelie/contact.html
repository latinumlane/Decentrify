How a commission runs

First — the conversation
An hour at the bench or a video call. Bring images, stones or an old piece.
Then — the drawing
Two options, drawn by hand, with a fixed price. No charge if you walk away.
Then — four weeks
Carving, casting, setting and polishing, with photographs as it goes.
After — for life
Cleaning, re-tipping and resizing stay free, for as long as you wear it.

Before you write

Questions we get most weeks

If the answer you need is not here, ask in the form above — it will not go unread.

Can you use my own gold?

Yes. We weigh it in front of you and credit it against the piece.

Do you ship abroad?

Insured, worldwide, two to five days. Duties are shown before you pay.

How long does a repair take?

Most leave within a week. Resizing while you wait, if the bench is free.

Is the first fitting free?

Always, and there is no obligation at the end of it.
